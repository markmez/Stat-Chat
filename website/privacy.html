Privacy Policy

StatChat by Second Signal, Inc.

Effective Date: March 16, 2026

Overview

StatChat ("the App") is a baseball statistics app developed by Second Signal, Inc. ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle data.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate and improve the App:

  • Device Identifier: A randomly generated anonymous ID stored on your device. This is used to manage your query quota and is not linked to your identity.
  • Query Text: The questions you ask in the App are sent to our servers to generate answers. We do not associate queries with your name, email, or any personal information.
  • Usage Analytics: We use Mixpanel to collect anonymous usage data such as which features are used, query types, and app opens. This data is tied to the anonymous device identifier and is used solely to improve the App. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information (unless you contact us for support).
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other personal data on your device.
  • We do not use advertising or sell data to third parties.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process your baseball questions and return accurate answers.
  • To manage free and subscription query limits.
  • To improve the App based on aggregate usage patterns.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Some queries are processed using Anthropic's Claude API to generate SQL queries and natural language answers. Query text is sent to Anthropic's servers. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Mixpanel: Anonymous usage analytics. See Mixpanel's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple (StoreKit): Subscription purchases are processed by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Query data processed by our backend servers is not persistently stored beyond the duration needed to generate a response. Anonymous analytics data is retained in Mixpanel according to their standard retention policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@secondsignalapps.com.
